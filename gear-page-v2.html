Record Cleaning

3 picks
Budget Pick

$15–$25

Carbon Fiber Anti-Static Brush

Non-negotiable for every setup. Run this across your record before every play. The bristles reach into grooves to pull dust out while the anti-static strip stops it from resettling immediately. Boundless Audio, Big Fudge, and AudioQuest all make solid versions. Buy one now.

Buy / Learn More ↗
Best Value

$80–$100

Spin Clean Record Washer System Mk II

The most popular manual wet-wash system out there. You spin the record through a bath of water and cleaning fluid while brushes scrub the grooves. No electricity, no moving parts. It genuinely removes embedded grime that a dry brush can't touch. New records especially benefit from a first wash.

Premium Pick

$599–$649

Pro-Ject VC-S3 Vacuum Record Cleaner

Motor-driven wet cleaning with a vacuum pickup arm. If you're serious about your collection, this is the one. You buy it once.
