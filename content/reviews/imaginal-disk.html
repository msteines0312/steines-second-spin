One of the greatest pop albums I have truly ever heard, whether through the infectious hooks on songs like Killing Time, Image or That's My Floor, or the deep and layered production on Cry for Me. This album ended up being my introduction to the duo, and since then they have been the heaviest constant in my rotation.

I find myself going through and giving this album a full listen once or twice a week. For my production-heads out there, do yourself a favor and put on Cry for Me right now with your greatest pair of headphones. Matt truly outdid himself with this song, through the synth-heavy intro and the "evil ABBA" sounding second half. As my brother once said, "it sounds like listening to Dancing Queen in Bowser's Castle."

Favorites: Cry For Me, The Ballad of Matt and Mica, Tunnel Vision
