For the first time in a while I wasn't horrifically disappointed by music that Kanye West put out. Unfortunately, this lack of disappointment comes from the realization that he simply might be incapable of ever reaching even half of his prior highs.

Bully comes after a ridiculous saga of PR nightmares, bigoted comments and a disgusting overreliance on AI in verses. Most of these songs sound like voicemails or ideas for songs that never got finished. Since 2018's Ye, Kanye has struggled to put together a cohesive tracklist, especially one where every song feels realized. Even in his 2021 release Donda, Kanye felt more like a manager than a true artist taking control of his album. Since my first listen I have found myself only going back to one track: the Travis Scott collab, FATHER. Nothing else had the pull to bring me back.

Favorite: FATHER
