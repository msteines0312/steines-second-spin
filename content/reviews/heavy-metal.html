Geese frontman Cameron Winter delivers an absolutely incredible album in his 2024 release, Heavy Metal. Despite not having the high-energy and angst that can be found throughout most of the Geese music, Cameron dominates this calmer and "wacky" lane. Due to the longer song-lengths and more lyrical focus, Cameron is truly able to flex his writing skills on this album, writing some absolutely heartwrenching lyrics.

It took me a couple listens to truly connect with this album, mostly due to some of the more interesting production decisions, however once it stuck I've found it to be incredibly prevalent in my rotation. These aren't exactly songs you play around other people, you surely don't need to listen to these in the car to get pumped, but when you want to listen to some truly incredible music this album fits like a glove.

Favorites: Love Takes Miles, Cancer of the Skull, $0
