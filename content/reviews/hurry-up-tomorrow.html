The highly anticipated final album from The Weeknd, wrapping up both his time as The Weeknd as well as the Divine Trilogy of albums that began in 2020 with After Hours. Abel luckily delivers on both fronts, creating a cohesive and cinematic experience that I still find myself regularly coming back to a year later.

I have to admit I come at this review with a lot of bias, as I have been known to be a bit of a "superfan" of Abel. He has been my number one artist on Spotify Wrapped every year, and throughout all three of his last major releases, that album was my most listened to of the year. Despite my obvious favoritism, I can comfortably say this album and the experience it brings is truly fantastic, whether it was the ensuing tour, music videos, or accompanying movie (ok maybe not this one...).

Not only does Abel deliver, but this album features arguably his best gallery of guests on any album he has done. Whether Future, Lana Del Rey, or production from JUSTICE. Truly an album for the ages, and one I feel will solidify itself as one of the greats from the 2020s.

Favorites: Without A Warning, Red Terror, The Abyss
