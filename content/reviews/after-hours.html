Probably one of the most influential albums of my teen years, as it was released exactly one week after the Covid lockdown began. A masterpiece from start to finish, and the beginning of my favorite trilogy in music ever. Abel absolutely outdid himself on every level for this album, through the music itself, the music videos and the whole aesthetic theme along with it. Although Blinding Lights got incredibly overplayed, it was hard not to get into the groove when you saw Abel in that now iconic red suit.

A return to form in many ways for The Weeknd, as his previous full-length releases Starboy and Beauty Behind The Madness remain at the lowest of his catalog in my opinion. We finally get the long running, dark and mysterious production that he built his career off of on Trilogy and Kissland, with his seemingly new synth-pop feel. Truly a reinventive album that seemed to influence many artists at the time, as the "80's" album archetype became incredibly prevalent around that period.

Abel makes it abundantly clear that he doesn't need any features to deliver a masterpiece of an album, as aside from some Tame Impala vocals on the interlude, you only hear Abel's voice. His magnum opus and his breakthrough to world-wide stardom. If you haven't listened yet, make sure you give it a listen.

Favorites: After Hours, Until I Bleed Out, Faith
