An album that truly sounds all over the place, but so clear and confident in the band's personal sound. Getting Killed was my introduction to Geese and the incredible world of Cameron Winter. This album truly does have a little something for everyone.

In the end of 2025, it was nearly impossible to look anywhere on social media and not be bombarded with Cameron Winter and Geese "propaganda." I am here to say that it's both well deserved and worth giving the album a listen.

The album's opener, Trinidad, features a high energy, aggressive hook from Cameron Winter (and background vocals from JPEGMAFIA). Whether it's the screaming, the heavy drums, or the perfect mix between the different instruments, Geese really knows how to open up an album. While Cameron and Geese are known for more "in-your-face" music, they do truly hit some unfamiliar calm notes on songs such as Cobra and the devastating Au Pays Du Cocaine.

Favorites: Bow Down, Au Pays Du Cocaine, Trinidad
