Kevin Parker's recent venture as Tame Impala after a long five years. Deadbeat proved to be one of his most divisive works yet. Despite having the traditional Tame Impala sounds, the album was met with mixed reviews: some claiming it to be too generic and boring compared to his older work, while others viewed it as him expanding upon the glossy and ethereal sounds from his prior album The Slow Rush.

As many artists do, Kevin has attempted to recharge his sound through every album, which has turned off quite a lot of the older fans who prefer his rock sound from his first two albums. For these fans, this album is a jarring change to what they were expecting. Songs such as Not My World, Ethereal Connection, and End Of Summer serve as Kevin letting his electronic tendencies run wild, especially on the lengthy Ethereal Connection.

Truly an album that once you "catch the groove" and get yourself into the space of it, you can appreciate everything he tried on here.

Favorites: Not My World, Afterthought, Dracula
