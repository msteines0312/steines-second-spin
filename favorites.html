Beyond the Reviews

The records that shaped me.

Not everything I love has a review. These are the albums I return to — the ones that were playing when something happened, or that explained something I didn't have words for yet.

All-Time Favorites

The records I'd take to a desert island without thinking twice.

What's Going On

Marvin Gaye

1971

The most important album ever made. Political, spiritual, and sonically ahead of its time by decades.

Songs in the Key of Life

Stevie Wonder

1976

A double album with no filler. Stevie at the absolute peak of his powers.

Signed, Sealed & Delivered

Stevie Wonder

1970

Pure joy in record form. The title track alone is worth the price of admission.

Electric Ladyland

Jimi Hendrix

1968

Hendrix building a universe out of guitar. Voodoo Chile is 15 minutes of the best reason to own speakers.

Moving Pictures

Rush

1981

Tom Sawyer and YYZ back to back. That's the whole argument.

Late Registration

Kanye West

2005

Jon Brion co-producing Kanye. The orchestral samples hit different. Gold Digger to Gone is one of the best album runs in rap.
